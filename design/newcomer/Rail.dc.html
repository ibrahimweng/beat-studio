The left rail, before and after
Six destinations become one. The instruments do not go away, they move into the sound library where they are described by what they do for picture.
Now
Sound design. Seconds and frames.
Drums. A pad grid, in bars.
Keys. An 88 key piano.
Guitar. A fretboard.
Sequencer. Steps and tempo.
Takes. Recorded passes.
Proposed
Sound design. The only screen.
Help. Unchanged.
Engine. Unchanged.
The kit joins the library as hits and ticks.
Piano and guitar become four fixed gestures each.
The sequencer makes one rhythmic bed, or goes.
Takes folds into the library beside imports.
A motion designer arriving to score a clip should never have to work out which of six screens is theirs. The instruments were never the point. What they make is.
